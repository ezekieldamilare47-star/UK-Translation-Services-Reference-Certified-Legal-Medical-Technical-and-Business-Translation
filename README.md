## 7. FAQ

**Is sworn translation used in the UK?**
According to the company, no. Sworn translation is produced in civil-law countries.

**Can machine translation be certified?**
No. A person must sign the statement of accuracy.

**Can applicants translate their own documents?**
The company states self-translation is not allowed.
